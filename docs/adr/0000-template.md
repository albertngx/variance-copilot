## Context

What problem or force makes a decision necessary? Include constraints, requirements (with SPEC § references) and what happens if nothing is decided.

## Decision

What we will do, stated in full sentences. Number the parts if there are several.

## Consequences

**Positive**

- What becomes easier or better.

**Negative / risks**

- What becomes harder, what we give up, and how risks are mitigated.
